Help & Support

Welcome to RecipeHub! Here’s a quick guide to help you use our website.

🔍 How to Search for Recipes

Use the search bar at the top of the homepage to find recipes by name, ingredient, or cuisine.

📁 How to Browse Categories

Click on “Categories” in the navigation menu to explore recipes by type, such as Desserts, Vegan, or Quick Meals.

📝 How to Submit Your Own Recipe

Go to the “Submit Recipe” page, fill out the form with your recipe details, and click “Submit”. Our team will review and publish it soon!

💬 Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Do I need an account to save recipes?

A: Yes, you’ll need to sign up or log in to save your favorite recipes.

Q: Can I print a recipe?

A: Yes! Open any recipe and click the “Print” button at the top of the page.

Q: Is there a mobile app?

A: Not yet, but our website is fully mobile-responsive.
